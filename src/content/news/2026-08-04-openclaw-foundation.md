OpenClaw has taken its biggest structural step yet: the project is now a formally incorporated 501(c)(3) non-profit organization serving a global community. The OpenClaw Foundation brings with it a full-time team, major corporate partnerships, and a stated mission to steward the project as open and independent for the long term.

**From Weekend Project to Global Movement**

Six months ago, OpenClaw was a single developer's side project. Today, the project is generating 4.5 million new activations per week and has become, by some measures, the fastest-growing repository in GitHub history. That growth prompted the formalization.

"The foundation exists to protect OpenClaw and steward it as open and independent," the announcement reads. "The great open source projects of our time — Linux, Apache, Mozilla — endure because a neutral steward stands behind them."

**The Full-Time Team**

For the first time, OpenClaw has dedicated staff. The engineering team includes Vincent Koc as Chief Architect, Josh Avant, Patrick Erichsen, Dallin Romney, Jason Sy, and Gideon Adegbesan. Operations is led by Jen Vescio (Partnerships), Matt Jasie (Finance), Hannes Rudolph (Community), and Kelly Pike (Talent). Hiring is ongoing across engineering, design, developer relations, and marketing.

**Major Partnerships**

The Foundation announcement detailed several high-profile partnerships:

- **OpenAI** has committed to keeping OpenClaw open and independent, is a major donor to the Foundation, and hosts Claw Labs — a team inside OpenAI led by Peter Steinberger working on shared product improvements.
- **NVIDIA** launched [NemoClaw](https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/ai/nemoclaw/) at GTC: one command installs OpenClaw with open Nemotron models and the OpenShell secure runtime for private, always-on AI on your own hardware. Industrial leaders including Cadence, Siemens, Synopsys, and Dassault Systèmes are already building autonomous AI engineers on NemoClaw. Jensen Huang's assessment at GTC: "Every company in the world today needs to have an OpenClaw strategy."
- **Microsoft** announced [Microsoft Scout](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/06/02/introducing-microsoft-scout-your-always-on-personal-agent/) at Build — an enterprise-grade always-on autopilot agent powered by OpenClaw open-source technology, with Microsoft contributing directly upstream.
- **University of Michigan** is the Foundation's largest donor and launched the Institute for Agentic Computing, an academic hub for OpenClaw research including support for national initiatives like The Genesis Mission.

**Neutral Ground in the Agentic Era**

The Foundation's stated ambition is to be "the Switzerland of AI" — neutral ground where every model and lab can collaborate on standards in the era of agents. That work is already underway through Foundation-convened councils on agent identity, agent profiles, evals, and enterprise deployment.

MIT licensed, open, and independent — the OpenClaw Foundation is now the official steward.
