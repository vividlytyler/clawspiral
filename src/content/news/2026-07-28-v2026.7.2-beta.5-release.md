OpenClaw 2026.7.2-beta.5 landed today with a significant focus on **state safety and recovery**, addressing one of the most-requested reliability improvements for production deployments.

## Key Highlights

**State Safety and Recovery**
- Quarantine store that survives primary-database damage
- Crash-recoverable SQLite snapshots
- Crash-durable filesystem publication
- Schema-upgrade data-loss rejection
- Rollback-writer snapshot recovery

**Durable Channel Delivery**
Messages across Telegram, Signal, Slack, QQBot, Twitch, Synology Chat, Tlon, IRC, and Zalo User are now kept recoverable across gateway restarts and local crashes through a shared ingress drain and dead-letter recovery system.

**Session Rewind and Branching**
Users can now rewind or fork conversations from individual messages, switch transcript branches across web and native apps, fork upstream Codex sessions, and restore prompt images after a fork.

**Interactive MCP Apps and Dashboards**
The release introduces hosted MCP Apps with bound tools, resources, and bounded context updates—openable from channel replies and pinnable to durable dashboards.

**Questions and Approvals Everywhere**
Agents can now ask structured questions with option cards across web, channels, macOS, and native apps. Approvals gain push notifications, history, fair queuing, headless resolution, and Claude tool-request relay.

The full changelog and download links are available on [GitHub](https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw/releases).
